## How status is decided

- **Content**: each exam objective has a short checklist of the terms its bullet list names. The share of those terms found in the linked CIWT lessons and labs is the *topic ratio*: covered at ≥ 70%, partial at ≥ 25%, otherwise a gap. A lesson is linked when it sits in a chapter the curated map assigns to the objective and mentions its terms, or mentions many of them anywhere.
- **Items**: covered with ≥ 4 aligned bank items, partial with 1–3.
- **Overall**: covered only when content and items are both covered; a gap when content is a gap; otherwise partial.
- This is a keyword heuristic, not a judgement of depth or accuracy. Every pass-11 addition is tagged *Pending SME review*.
- CompTIA publishes weights only per domain. The per-objective weights below divide each domain weight equally across its objectives; that is an assumption, used only to rank the work.
