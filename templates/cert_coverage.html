How status is decided

Content is measured with a keyword checklist per objective (our own list of the terms the objective names): covered when at least 70 % of the checklist appears in aligned lessons and labs, partial from 25 %, otherwise a gap. Assessment is covered with at least 4 aligned bank items. An objective is covered only when both are; it is a gap when the content is.

This is a heuristic: a term appearing does not prove it is taught to exam depth. New content added for alignment is tagged Pending SME review. Per-objective weights shown are the published domain weight shared equally across the domain’s objectives — CompTIA publishes domain weights only.
