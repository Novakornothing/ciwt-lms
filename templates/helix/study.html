Helix · accuracy study (optional)

Help measure how accurate webcam eye tracking really is

Two short accuracy checks on your own device, about 1–2 minutes in total. Not part of any grade.

What is recorded, and what is not

Each check shows dots at known places on the screen while you look at them. Helix compares where it estimated you were looking with where the dot really was. Only these numbers are saved:

  • the error in pixels and in degrees, precision, a validity flag and the accuracy score;
  • for each of the 5 check dots: its position on the screen (as a fraction), the median error and the number of estimates;
  • how long calibration took and which method was used (dots or follow-the-dot, standard or 1080p camera);
  • device class (desktop / tablet / phone), browser name and major version, camera video size, window size, pixel ratio, estimated distance from the screen, and the gaze engine.

Never recorded: camera images or video, face landmarks, raw gaze samples, or anything about lesson content. The server refuses uploads that look like images or landmarks.

Your account id is kept with each record only so you can delete your records. Admins see results grouped by device, browser and camera resolution, never per person. Instructors do not see study records.
