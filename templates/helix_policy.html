Written product policy

Helix attention is a signal, not an assessment

Version {{ policy_version }} · Engine: WebGazer in the student browser · Face video is not uploaded

What this feature is

When a student opts in on a lesson page, the browser runs WebGazer and estimates where the pointer of gaze sits on the page. The LMS stores a compact area-of-interest (AOI) timeline for that student and lesson: which headings or blocks the estimate lingered on, for how long, and in what order.

Instructors can open a class dashboard built from those timelines. The dashboard exists so a teacher can notice “this block got almost no dwell” or “this student skipped the lab practice heading,” then decide whether to reteach, check in, or leave it alone.

What this feature is not
  • Not a grade, score, GPA input, or knowledge-test substitute.
  • Not attendance, not “time on task” for pay or discipline, not a proctoring camera for exams.
  • Not proof that a student understood, cheated, or was inattentive. Calibration error, glasses, lighting, posture, looking at notes, and looking at a lab window all move the signal.
  • Not a recording of the student’s face. Video is processed locally so gaze can be estimated. The server stores AOI keys, labels, dwell, and segment times — not pixels.
Rules for schoolhouse use
  1. Participation is opt-in per lesson session. A student who clicks “Not now” still gets the full curriculum, labs, quizzes, and tests.
  2. Instructors and admins may view class and per-student AOI timelines for sections they already have access to.
  3. Do not put dwell percentages on a score sheet, promotion package, or counseling chit.
  4. Do not require gaze as a condition of sitting a knowledge test.
  5. Treat thin dwell as a prompt to ask a question or point at a heading — the same way you would treat a quiet room after a lecture slide.
  6. Students may stop the camera at any time. Stopping ends the session and flushes the last segments.

If a command or contract requires a different rule, write that rule down and keep this page as the product default until it is replaced.
