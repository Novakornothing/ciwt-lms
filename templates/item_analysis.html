NAVEDTRA test-item analysis techniques
  • Difficulty index (P) = Nc / N (proportion correct). Higher P = easier item. Acceptable band for high pass scores (~80%): about {{ summary.p_band or '0.60–0.95' }}.
  • Discrimination index (d) = Ph − Pl using upper vs lower ~27% by total score. Prefer d ≥ 0.30; negative d flags review.
  • Effectiveness of alternatives — each distractor’s share of responses; non-functional alternatives (<5% chosen) should be revised.
  • Low throughput: full sample used; items missed by ≥50% are flagged for review.

Aligned to NETC instructional testing practice (difficulty, discrimination, alternatives). Not a substitute for official course of instruction approval processes.
