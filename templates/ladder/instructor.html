After each lesson section students get a quick check (not graded). A miss, or “I don't get it”, replaces that section, for that student only, with the next explanation: more simply, an analogy, a picture or trace, then something to try. Grades are never changed. All alternative explanations are Pending SME review.

Settings for this class

Off: students can still press “I don't get it”.
Off (default): every student sees the original first. On: a student with a clear pattern (see the rule on their page) sees their best kind of explanation first, with “See the original” one tap away.
On (default): “Next lesson” waits until every section's quick check is answered correctly, or every explanation was tried (then you are flagged in Who needs me, and the student can continue). Never graded.
