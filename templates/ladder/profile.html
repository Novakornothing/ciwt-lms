How this is decided
A kind of explanation is offered first for {{ 'them' if staff else 'you' }} only when at least {{ P.PROFILE_MIN_RESOLVED }} sections, from at least {{ P.PROFILE_MIN_LESSONS }} lessons, were cleared by another explanation; that kind was tried on at least {{ P.PROFILE_MIN_SHOWN }} sections and cleared at least {{ P.PROFILE_MIN_BEST }}; it worked at least {{ (P.PROFILE_MIN_RATE * 100)|int }}% of the times it was tried; and that is at least {{ (P.PROFILE_MIN_RATE_LEAD * 100)|int }} points better than every other kind tried as often. Rates, not counts, because “more simply” is always tried first. Even then the original explanation is still shown first{% if cfg.adapt_first %}, except that the instructor of this class turned on “adapt the first presentation”{% endif %}; the preferred kind is simply the first alternative offered.
