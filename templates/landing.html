What does DNS do?

DNS resolves a hostname such as example.com to an IP address. The client asks a resolver, which queries root, TLD, and authoritative servers, then caches the answer for its TTL.

Computers find each other by number, not by name. DNS turns the name you type into the number the computer needs.

DNS is your phone's contact list. You tap "Mom"; the phone dials the number saved under that name.

FAQ

What is Novak LMS™?

A learning platform with three courses (IT Support, Basic Networking, Operating Systems), 5 Ways to Learn on every lesson, browser labs, and Practice Exams.

Does Eye Tracking record video?

No. Eye Tracking is optional and off by default. It runs in the browser, and no video or images ever leave the device. Students can view or delete their data at /my-data.

Is Novak LMS™ affiliated with CompTIA?

No. Courses are aligned to published exam objectives but are independent Novak LMS™ material.

How do colleges or training commands run a pilot?

Start a free 30-seat pilot at /pilot/start, or enroll students by join code, CSV, or LTI 1.3.

Where is the privacy note?

Privacy note: we keep only what a course needs, FERPA-aligned. Sensitive actions go into a tamper-evident audit log. Read the Privacy page or email novakornothing@gmail.com.
