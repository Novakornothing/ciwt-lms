Technical FAQ & Legal Information

What is Novak LMS™?

An adaptive LMS with Client Systems (FC0-U71), IT Support (220-1201 / 220-1202), and Networking (N10-009) courses, 115 visually blueprinted lessons (639,146 total instructional words across 242,549 base words and 539 4-rung adaptive ladders), 90 hands-on labs baseline (expanded to 105 total labs including 8 Ubuntu GNOME GUI workbenches and 24 real in-browser Linux VMs), and a 1,400-item Exam-Day Simulator with 36 3-Tab Interactive Visual PBQs.

How does Novak LMS™ compare to CompTIA CertMaster, Cisco NetAcad, and Pearson VUE-style platforms?

First-party static certware splits learning, labs, and practice into 3 separate paid SKUs and serves the same static text when a student struggles; Novak LMS™ unifies 639,146 words of instruction with 100% lesson SVG visual blueprints, 539 in-place 4-rung adaptive explanation ladders (R1–R4), 90 browser labs (24 real WebAssembly Linux VMs) graded on Diagnose → Fix → Verify process, 36 3-Tab Interactive Visual PBQ Workbenches, 1,400 Exam-Sim items, 405 proctored Knowledge Test questions across Forms A/B/C, per-objective Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, and 100% on-device Helix gaze telemetry.

How do the 3-Tab Interactive Visual PBQ Workbench and 90 hands-on labs work?

All 36 multi-part simulation PBQs run inside a 3-Tab Interactive Visual PBQ Workbench: (1) a clickable SVG Topology & Hardware Visualizer, (2) a Live Device Inspector exposing show interfaces trunk, show ip ospf neighbor, SMART/POST, bcdedit /enum, icacls, and ls -la logs, and (3) a working pbq-cli diagnostic terminal. Complementing the PBQs are 105 hands-on labs (built on our 90 browser-based labs baseline, including 24 real Alpine/x86 WebAssembly Linux VMs and 8 Ubuntu 24.04 GNOME GUI workbenches) graded on engineering process (Diagnose → Fix → Verify).

How deep is the Novak LMS™ curriculum and exam question bank?

Novak LMS™ covers 3 full courses, 26 chapters, and 115 lessons totaling 242,549 base lesson words and 639,146 total instructional words (including 396,597 words across 539 4-rung adaptive explanation ladders), 1,400 unique scenario-based Exam-Sim items across 100% of the 98 published sub-objectives, 36 interactive simulation PBQs, 405 proctored Knowledge Test questions across Forms A, B, and C, and 325 Speed-Drill flashcards.

How does Novak LMS™ combine Helix eye tracking and Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) for instructors?

Novak LMS™ runs a per-objective Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) mastery engine combining lesson checks, chapter quizzes, live lab telemetry (weight 0.85), Knowledge Tests, and Exam-Sim sittings (weight 0.85). Alongside BKT, students can opt in per session to Helix Attention Telemetry—a browser-local 478-landmark MediaPipe FaceMesh iris and 3D head-pose gaze tracker with velocity-adaptive One-Euro filtering (OneEuro1D), 100% on-device in volatile RAM with zero video transmitted. The 10-second "Who Needs Me" Instructor Co-Pilot synthesizes these signals to show which students are stuck, on which exact objective, and what ladder rung (R1–R4) was already served.

How does Helix attention tracking protect student privacy?

It is opt-in per session with explicit consent, runs entirely in the browser and sends only summary numbers (such as time on a section), and no video or images ever leave the device. Students can switch it off at any time.

How do colleges or training commands run a pilot?

Provision a self-service 30-Day / 30-Seat Institutional Pilot in 60 seconds at /pilot/start, or enroll students via join code, CSV, SCORM 1.2/2004, or LTI 1.3 (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Brightspace).
