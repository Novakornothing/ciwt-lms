How does Novak LMS™ combine Helix eye tracking and Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) for instructors?

Novak LMS™ runs a per-objective Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) mastery engine combining lesson checks, chapter quizzes, live lab telemetry (weight 0.85), Knowledge Tests, and Exam-Sim sittings (weight 0.85). Alongside BKT, students can opt in per session to Helix Attention Telemetry—a browser-local 478-landmark MediaPipe FaceMesh iris and 3D head-pose gaze tracker with velocity-adaptive One-Euro filtering (OneEuro1D), saccade step-boost, and outlier-trimmed calibration (100% on-device, zero video transmitted, FERPA-aligned). The 10-second "Who Needs Me" Instructor Co-Pilot synthesizes these signals to show which students are stuck, on which exact objective, what ladder rung (R1–R4) was already served, and where gaze dwell or rereading indicates cognitive friction.

How does the in-lesson explanation ladder work?

Every concept card gives students 5 freely clickable learning paths at the top of the card—Standard (R0), Plain English (R1), Real-World Analogy (R2), Visual Map (R3), and Interactive Try-It (R4)—so any learner can switch modalities on demand at any time. Each of the 4 balanced Concept Steps per lesson ends with a Milestone Check (while sub-sections offer optional practice self-checks); a miss automatically escalates to the next explanation rung in place.

How does Helix attention tracking protect student privacy?

It is opt-in per session with explicit consent, runs entirely in the browser and sends only summary numbers (such as time on a section), never video or images. Students can switch it off at any time.
