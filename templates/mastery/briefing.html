How this briefing is made

Everything comes from data already in Novak LMS™: mastery estimates and the decision log, “Who needs me”, mix-ups from tagged wrong answers, lab event logs, consented Helix reading data, recent chapter checks and tests, releases and check-ins. Fixed rules rank the to-do list; each item shows its priority and how it was counted. The same data always gives the same briefing. No AI model writes any of it.
