How the estimate works

Each objective starts at {{ (model_params.p_init * 100)|round|int }}%. Every answer moves it: a right answer raises it, a wrong one lowers it, allowing for lucky guesses ({{ (model_params.guess * 100)|round|int }}%) and slips ({{ (model_params.slip * 100)|round|int }}%). Graded test and mastery-check questions count fully, chapter checks {{ (weights.quiz * 100)|round|int }}%, review checks {{ (weights.review_check * 100)|round|int }}% and lab results {{ (weights.lab * 100)|round|int }}%. Reading a lesson for at least half its time adds a little; reading less never takes anything away.
