2. Standardized DMCA Notice & Take-Down Protocol (17 U.S.C. § 512)

17 U.S.C. § 512(c) Safe Harbor Compliant

Novak Learning respects the intellectual property rights of others and strictly enforces a standardized Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA") Notice-and-Takedown Protocol pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(c).

2.1 Filing a Valid DMCA Take-Down Notice

If you are a copyright owner (or authorized agent) and believe in good faith that any material hosted on Novak LMS™ infringes your copyright, please send a written DMCA Take-Down Notice containing all six (6) statutory elements required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) to our Designated Copyright Agent:

  1. Signature: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Work Identified: Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a representative list if multiple copyrighted works at a single online site are covered by a single notification).
  3. Exact URL / Locator: Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity and that is to be removed or access to which is to be disabled, including the exact Novak LMS™ URL(s), course/section ID, or lesson/item identifier sufficient to permit Novak Learning to locate the material.
  4. Contact Information: Information reasonably sufficient to permit Novak Learning to contact the complaining party, such as a mailing address, telephone number, and electronic mail address.
  5. Good-Faith Statement: A statement that the complaining party has a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Accuracy & Perjury Statement: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
